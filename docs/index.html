60 FPS presentation

60 pictures a second through Vulkan, with an in-between picture drawn between every two game frames. The game itself runs at 30, as on the PSP, so timing and combos are unchanged.

Lighting & shaders

Up to 6× internal resolution, texture upscaling, MSAA and anisotropic filtering. A post-processing pass adds bloom, contrast, warmth, sharpening and ambient occlusion, and stages get lighting set per stage.

Rollback netcode & lobbies

Quick Match, Ranked (FT2) with Soul Points and Soul Rate, public and private rooms, Live Matches spectating, Fighter Cards and Direct Connect.

Replay Theater

Save any battle from its results screen with SELECT, play it back from the REPLAYS card, and export it straight to an MP4 video.

Training suite

Frame data, input display, dummy recording and playback, Ghost Sparring, playback speed down to 10%, and instant position reset.

Battle Royale, restored

Four-player Battle Royale on every versus stage, with each fighter's alternate colours.

Automatic updates

Checks GitHub Releases at start and sends nothing about you. A newer release shows a quiet pill on the main menu. The download is verified (size and SHA-256) before it installs, and saves and settings are never touched.

PC controls & mods

XInput and keyboard with remapping and button icons, a BGM Jukebox with custom soundtrack packs, and built-in texture dumping with HD replacement.

The same frame of the same scripted battle, rendered by the port as the PSP draws it (480×272, software) and as the PC recompilation draws it (4× internal resolution, Vulkan). Drag the handle.

PSP · 480×272PC · v1.02
  1. Download the .zip from the latest release (no account needed) and extract it to its own folder. Do not run it from inside the zip.
  2. Add your game files. Put your decrypted EBOOT.BIN of UCJS-10110 in the game\ folder (game\README.txt explains how to dump it with PPSSPP). Keep your disc image (.iso) anywhere.
  3. Play. Double-click BleachNative.exe. The DISC SETUP screen asks for your .iso and checks both files; press START to launch.

Needs 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 and a Vulkan graphics driver. QUICKSTART.txt in the release covers controls, settings, online play, replays and troubleshooting. Updates install from inside the game: PC Settings ▸ Check for Updates.
